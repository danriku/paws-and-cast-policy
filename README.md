<h2>1. Introduction</h2>

Welcome to Paws & Cast: Idle Fishing Cat, developed by Danriku Studio. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you play our mobile game.

<h2>2. Information Collection and Use</h2>

- <b>Personal Information </b>: We do not directly collect, request, or store personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number).
- <b>Game Progress & Save Data</b>: All gameplay data, including your inventory, unlocked cat upgrades, and fishing progress, is stored locally on your mobile device. Uninstalling the app or clearing local app storage will permanently reset your save data.

<h2>3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)</h2>

The Game integrates Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect and process certain technical data from your device to deliver, personalize, and measure ads.

This data may include:
- Unique Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, and device hardware specifications.
- Usage & Ad Interaction Data: Advertisements viewed, clicks, and diagnostic performance logs.
- Coarse Location Data: Approximate geographic location derived from your IP address.

Google AdMob uses this information in accordance with its own privacy practices. For details on how Google handles collected data, please review <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy">Google’s Privacy & Terms.</a>

<h2>4. Children's Privacy</h2>

Our game is designed to be family-friendly and accessible to players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ad requests sent via third-party advertising partners are configured to comply with applicable children's privacy regulations (including COPPA) by disabling personalized tracking for child audiences.

<h2>5. Data Security</h2>

We prioritize the security of your device data. Aside from the automated network traffic required by third-party advertising SDKs (AdMob), no personal user data is transmitted to or stored on our own external servers.

<h2>6. Changes to This Privacy Policy</h2>

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app's features or legal compliance requirements. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
